# Task 1: Product Thinking

## Product Chosen: Spotify

Spotify is one of my favorite apps because of its clean interface, personalized recommendations, and seamless music streaming experience. While it offers many useful features, I believe there is room for improvement.

## Feature I Would Introduce: Mood-Based Smart Playlists

I would introduce an **AI-powered Mood-Based Smart Playlist** feature.

The idea is simple: instead of users manually searching for songs or playlists, Spotify could generate playlists based on the user's current mood. Users could select emotions such as **Happy, Relaxed, Focused, Energetic, Sad, or Stressed**, and Spotify would instantly create a personalized playlist using their listening history and recommendation engine.

To make the feature even better, users could optionally connect it with data from wearable devices (such as heart rate or activity level) or use simple mood check-ins to improve playlist recommendations over time.

### Why This Feature?

- Makes discovering music faster and more personalized.
- Reduces the effort of searching for the right playlist.
- Improves user engagement by adapting to changing moods.
- Helps users relax, focus, or stay motivated with suitable music.

## Feature I Would Remove or Improve

I would improve the **Spotify Queue** system.

Currently, when users add songs to the queue while listening to playlists or albums, it can become confusing because queued songs and upcoming playlist songs are mixed together.

I would separate them into two sections:

- **Queued Songs** – Songs manually added by the user.
- **Playlist Queue** – Songs that are scheduled to play automatically from the current playlist or album.

This would give users better control over their listening experience and make the queue easier to understand.

## Conclusion

Spotify already provides an excellent music streaming experience, but adding an AI-powered mood-based playlist feature and improving the queue management system would make it even more personalized, user-friendly, and engaging.
